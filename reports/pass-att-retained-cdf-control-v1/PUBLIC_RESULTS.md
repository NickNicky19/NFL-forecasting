# Restored 2026 passing-attempt CDF control

## What was actually completed

A retained, authenticated CDF model generated new historical reconstruction forecasts with its existing parameters. No model was refitted and no new prior was estimated. Both full 2,999-row canonical-law/API diagnostic views were frozen before evaluation labels were loaded. The original blocked comparator remains blocked under its original identity; this separately identified reconstruction does not reproduce an unretained original forecast vector.

The first run saved the forecasts and exhausted its compute allocation before saving a complete grading result. A separately registered grading-only continuation reused those exact forecasts, with no model load or prediction rerun. Both attempts and their consumed budgets remain recorded. Independent read-only review reproduced the saved row identities, points, outcomes, errors and paired arithmetic.

## Adverse findings and uncertainty

- CDF underprediction was worse: bias -1.422216 attempts versus -0.829321 for pooled-eight.
- CDF lost to pooled-eight in Weeks 4 and 6. Its p95 absolute error improved.
- All 30 CDF fallback opportunities remain in coverage; supported fallback outcomes are included in system scoring.
- The 95% paired whole-game descriptive interval for CDF minus pooled-eight MSE was [-34.171052, -6.516612], using 500 PCG64 replicates, seed 1703 and linear percentiles. This is exposed development evidence with dependent weeks and selection among methods, not a confirmation certificate.
- The registered whole-week interval is N/A because six clusters are below that function’s eight-cluster requirement. This does not create a new eight-week prerequisite for the provisional release track.
- Missing participation limits population claims. No calibrated probabilities, market comparison or selected-prop ranking result was produced.

## Source and correctness limits

The method is the existing 32-tree empirical leaf CDF (depth 8, minimum leaf 30) with its fixed 95% empirical/5% prior law and existing preprocessing. The output is the law mean. Source history remains current-vintage reconstruction under the original assumed seven-day availability rule. This is neither original pregame issuance nor untouched confirmation.

The two reconstructed full query views differed on three rows by at most one binary64 ULP (maximum absolute difference 3.552713678800501e-15). No tolerance was used for admission, and the old strict blocked member was not relabeled as passed. This diagnostic does not authenticate the missing original blocked vector.

The completed older F1–F3 procedures are player-history averages; F3 fixed a 365-day half-life. F4 reused the workload relation. Their negative results remain unchanged. They are distinct from the still-uncompleted common-learner history-policy and role-information experiment.

## Decision

Use this reconstruction as a strong matched laboratory control. It is research-tested; this result alone is not an operational package, live selection, deployment, scientific promotion or a win against today’s website champion. All other categories retain their existing evidence and status. No category inherits this result.
