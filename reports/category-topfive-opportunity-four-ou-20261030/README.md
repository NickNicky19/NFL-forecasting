## What is being compared

P1 selects Over only when the original unrounded point exceeds its original
line, Under only when it is below, and abstains on equality or missingness.
Stored F/FC/H points compete with explicitly labeled market-line heuristics.
Always Over and Always Under controls use the same pools.

P2_F_FULL uses actual saved F Over/Under/push masses. Other P2 arms use their
saved conditional decisive probabilities; those are not complete distributions
and supply no invented push mass. Proper probability scores, fixed-side results
and point errors are separate products. Impossible-event log losses stay in
their denominators.

P3 reuses the exact original frozen paper selections, sides, prices and assumed
settlements. No new P3 policy, ranking or selection is made. Actual bookmaker
settlement rules remain unqualified, so this cannot establish authenticated
historical profitability.

Raw statistic direction differs from sportsbook settlement. A qualified
full-game count remains a statistical outcome after an early exit. An early
exit does not establish a void/refund rule; that bookmaker contract remains
unresolved. DNP, played zero, played missing and unknown remain distinct.
