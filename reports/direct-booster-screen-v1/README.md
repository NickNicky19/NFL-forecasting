This is neither prospective confirmation nor a historical website comparison:
original raw Sleeper and SITE forecasts are unavailable for this panel.

All 198 estimator attempts were consumed. There are 192 successful candidate/year
scored files, six retained XGBoost anytime-TD probability-validation failures,

they are not 192 independent validation samples. The primary cohorts span 815

appearances, depending on target. All offered, unknown, missing and fallback
opportunities remain in the full summaries.

The six XGBoost TD failures were subsequently recovered in a
[separate zero-fit technical derivative](../direct-xgboost-binary-recovery-v2/README.md).
Both recovered settings lose the CDF reference and earn no development
nomination. The original 216 slots and failures here remain unchanged.

Selecting the lowest new primary loss in each category gives five small gains
and six losses against the strong CDF control. The gains range from 0.0282% to
1.0140%; **no pipeline earns even this screen's development nomination**. Passing
attempts/completions/TDs/interceptions/yards and rushing yards all have higher
primary error than the CDF control even for their lowest-loss new setting.
Carries improve 1.0140% against CDF mean MSE but lose 0.0405% against role B.
Anytime-TD Brier improves 0.1462%; its nominal week interval crosses zero.

These selected minima are exposed selection results. They do not establish
superiority, a gain over SITE, or release readiness. A simpler control remains
the better-supported retained reference. The requested dynamic role/opportunity,
correction, distributional, stacking and calibration families remain distinct
experiments with unmeasured gains; see the [all-category intake](../../docs/ADDITIONAL_METHODS_INTAKE_20261023.md).
