## New permitted-source experiment

The old cache contains PFR snap-derived participation and rolling inputs. A
specific grant resolving the retained PFR AI/ML permission limit was not
established. The new experiment omits snap files and rebuilds player windows,
participation and team/opponent context from NFL statistics, eligible weekly
rosters, schedules and identity metadata. No old PFR-derived feature table is
used for new model training or inference. Existing forecast outputs remain
frozen benchmarks only; this does not requalify them for deployment.

The new historical packet retains 62,746 roster/stat rows for 2019–2025:
42,392 have positive attributed physical NFL event evidence and 20,354 have
unknown participation. Penalties, roster ACT/INA, zero-only stat rows and row
presence do not prove an appearance. Explicit raw zeros remain in their source;
conditional feature/fit values without separate participation proof stay missing.
Unknown rows consume rolling slots. Snap means are disabled/missing and their
observed-value counts are zero; those counts are not fabricated snap outcomes.
This sufficient-positive-evidence sample is not a complete participant panel.

All 396 original website player-games and 2,083 category offers remain planned.
The clean scoring view resolves 243 anytime-TD, 252 reception/receiving-yard,
96 rushing-yard, 69 rushing-attempt and 189 combined-yard outcomes; the earlier
view had 249,258,97,70,192 respectively. Each comparison recomputes errors on the
same qualified player-games. Original states remain separately retained. No
confirmed-DNP versus new-positive-event contradiction or lost quarantine exists;
the versioned grader fails closed if either arises. No future-week statistics
were read. Seven-day historical availability is assumed, not authenticated;
revision/roster advantages versus original forecasts remain possible.
