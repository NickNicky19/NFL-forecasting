# Execution and evidence specification

This document expands the short root Goal. It is an engineering/research assignment, not a finding that the following methods outperform the baseline. Solve the requested problem in working code rather than inflating the infrastructure.

## 1. What quality means

Aim for the strongest measured forecasts within permitted data and existing compute, not a model-brand certificate. Technical integration readiness, future predictive advantage, calibrated probabilities, and financial performance are different claims. No amount of testing makes outcomes certain or establishes an absolute global optimum over all possible methods.

Use the selected GPT-6.1 Sol Extra High for coordination/research. Preserve actual settings in run metadata. A separate read-only reviewer should reconstruct critical scores and probe leakage/failure cases. Review by a second agent is code/science quality assurance, NOT a second football sample. Agents use immutable data and bounded tasks, with at most four workers concurrently. One coordinator owns merges and shared registries. Reuse a common loader, identity registry, feature builder, evaluator and source cache instead of building twelve infrastructures.

## 2. Retain the actual earlier methods

The methods below are STARTING POINTS, not a fixed ceiling on future methods. Read retained reports for exact populations, sources, hashes, losses and fallback policies. Do not treat these short descriptions as recovered original executables.

- Passing yards: original website passing-attempt point multiplied by pooled passing yards/attempt over the exact three qualified earlier appearances; unchanged website fallback when inputs are unsupported. Reported n=27 over 13 games, MAE 56.622963 to 45.205603, RMSE 73.798344 to 66.822586, but signed bias worsened from -19.081481 to -24.072824. Candidate calculation occurred after the target games. Preserve the full cohort, including six fallbacks. Recover original runner and bindings; a new reconstruction cannot inherit the original reproduction claim.
- Passing attempts: preserve the small historical workload candidate's coefficients/preprocessing and historical comparator distinction. Its reported historical gains do not erase the mixed website comparison or the failed all-missing-input application. Do not silently substitute it into the pooled-YPA formula.
- Completions: original website attempts times pooled earlier completion fraction, with its own fallback. Reported website MAE improved while RMSE worsened. Retain both; do not choose the favored metric after results.
- Receptions: `rec-half-adjustment-v1 = raw + 0.5*(adjusted - raw)`, original registration `2026-10-16T19:36:46.224755Z`, 2026 Weeks 7-14, original earliest-common-timely-triple and source binding. Do not restart the window, fill missed forecasts, or tune its 0.5 weight. Coordinate ownership before claiming a second official recorder; isolated new experiments must remain distinct.
- Receiving yards: projected targets times pooled yards/target over three earlier appearances. The exact arithmetic core and tests are included. It assumes the historical triple was selected/authenticated elsewhere. Finish that adapter without skipping unknown or unfavorable intervening games. Test trade, bye, new-player and early-season fallbacks. The core has no demonstrated real forecasting gain.
- Rushing attempts: preserve the separate RB team-volume times shrunken-player-share proposal and original history/prior rules. Recover its protocol; do not manufacture its original fits or copy QB weights.
- Rushing yards: test workload and efficiency separately and jointly, retaining signed yards and conditional-participation versus all-player targets.
- Passing TDs/interceptions: opportunity/exposure-conditioned rare counts with pooling, zero mass, dispersion and participation. Distinguish passing TDs from rushing/receiving scorer events, and thrown from defensive interceptions.
- Combined yards: test compatible component means or coherent joint samples. Means add; medians/quantiles generally do not. A structural discrepancy is not proof that replacing the forecast with a sum improves it.
- Offensive PPR: exact pinned website scoring applied to compatible components/joint samples. Preserve fractional scores, fumbles, conversions and missing components; do not substitute a generic provider total.
- Anytime TD: define the website event as at least one rushing/receiving TD and separate participation/settlement. Existing descriptive probability skill is not evidence that arbitrary calibration offsets help. Use training-only calibration, later confirmation, and explicit missing outcomes.

## 3. Broader data and football mechanisms

Design candidate input groups, then keep them only when they demonstrate incremental value. Do not accumulate sources for their own sake.

A. Participation and opportunity: likely participation/starter status, injuries, roster and depth changes, recent usage, snaps/routes when actually available, carries, targets, red-zone opportunities, teammate availability and substitutions. Do not infer nonparticipation merely because a box-score row is missing. Separate injury uncertainty from an assumed zero.
B. Team/player ability: regularized player ability and role, team play volume, pace, pass/run allocation, player shares, passing/rushing/receiving efficiency. Compare recency windows and pooling in a bounded search; retain cold-start fallbacks and all eligibility strata.
C. Opponent and matchup: offense/defense strength adjusted for earlier opponents, position/role exposures, pressure/protection or personnel matchups when supported, coaching and coordinator changes. Distinguish rates from raw totals and ranks from underlying measurements. Fit ranks/ratings only on eligible earlier observations, with shrinkage for sparse opponents. Predict game-script scenarios rather than using realized score or play mix.
D. Scheme/gameplan proxies: historical pass tendency by down/distance/game state, personnel/formations, tempo, target depth, red-zone usage and permitted coverage/blitz information when actually observed. Use probabilistic expectations; do not invent access to private playbooks or assume a public comment is the actual future plan.
E. News: retain exact accessible source, publication/update/retrieval clocks, structured claim, relevant player/team, and confidence. Deduplicate repeated stories. Separate direct reported fact, coach statement, reporter interpretation, and model hypothesis. Test news as its own ablation; do not let an LLM narrative silently alter numerical forecasts. Historical news without an authentic pregame version is unsuitable for leakage-free historical feature claims.
F. Environment: pre-cutoff weather forecasts, venue/roof, rest, travel and home/away context. Realized target-game weather cannot substitute for its forecast without an explicit reconstructed-data limitation.
G. NGS/nflverse: use public supported aggregates and documented versions. Not all tracking, charting, routes or injuries are public/timely. nflverse documents late FTN participation, revision cycles and NGS-dependent updates. Preserve gaps and use permitted substitutes rather than assuming all source tables exist for all years.

Source missingness, data vintage and licensing are part of the model contract. Search official/public allowed sources and existing evidence before asking the owner for data already recoverable. Do not bypass controls or open paid services. A replacement source gets its own version and documented equivalence; it cannot silently repair an old original record.

## 4. Efficient experiments

Begin with a complete baseline-to-score slice rather than waiting for maximal features. Use the current website and raw provider only on properly paired observations; use simple pooled/recent controls where historical original forecasts are unavailable. Compare workload/efficiency, regularized/hierarchical, suitable tree models, distributional models and out-of-fold-weighted ensembles in a finite, reproducible ladder. Shared multitask structure is allowed when tested; twelve unrelated models are not mandatory. Select primary losses and probability semantics first.

Freeze each campaign's input versions, train/validation/confirmation boundaries, candidate budget, feature-group tests, stopping and promotion rules. Reuse cached feature matrices; checkpoint resumable fits. Diagnose numerical failure without choosing only successful/favorable runs. Run cheap correctness tests during edits and full independent acceptance on candidate versions; do not rerun identical suites solely to generate another count. Do not tune on already-exposed test results or repeatedly check the holdout until a preferred result appears. Use new later data for a new confirmation round; define the adaptive weekly procedure itself.

Record all attempted models, feature ablations, seeds, fitted parameters, environment and elapsed/resource use. Shared-cause failures should be fixed once in a common adapter. Missing an optional feature does not stop otherwise valid categories or baseline issuance.

## 5. Evaluation and operational cadence

Forecast before kickoff and persist before the declared cutoff, not just start computing before it. Preserve forecast and input timestamps. Schedule research jobs separately in this repository with one durable writer per state; never dispatch the website updater. Daily target may be 08:36 America/Chicago, but actual data/cutoff checks govern eligibility. A late run skips affected pregame issuance rather than backdating. Grading can run daily after supported final statistics arrive and again on later corrections; this grades every supported completed game, not necessarily instantly after its final whistle. No false 90-minute capture claim for a morning snapshot.

Score point metrics in target units, proper distribution/probability scores when valid, reliability/calibration and interval/tail coverage. Separate player-game accuracy from chosen-side hit rate and financial settlement. Bind any recorded line to its original player/game/side/book/time/rules and push state. An odds snapshot is not a permanent feed. No future line grading without a recorded future line.

Report category, player/role, team, opponent, prior defense-strength group and lead time with sample counts, missingness and fallback frequency. Use paired game/week dependence in uncertainty, appropriate multiple-comparison controls, and hierarchical/shrunken subgroup estimates. A high-performing tiny subgroup is exploratory, not a production selection policy. Measure both matched evaluation and total offered/forecast population so coverage changes cannot masquerade as accuracy gains.

Train/compare weekly under bounded no-extra-cost compute/storage. Existing method refits can be deterministic. New features/algorithms require new versioned research code and predeclared comparisons, not arbitrary source-code changes during every daily job. If a supported budgeted agent review is available, it can run a bounded research assignment; never assume a ChatGPT subscription grants free arbitrary LLM API calls. No unattended infinite LLM or Ticker loop.

Retire the previous ChatGPT research execution only after the new lab genuinely recovers the work and its replacement operation is verified. The lab must not claim it disabled an external task without supported control/readback. Do not create two active official recorders of the same frozen experiment.

## 6. Blocker resolution and authority

The short Goal authorizes lab implementation, scoped code/workflow changes, permitted input acquisition, experiment execution, merges, and bounded no-extra-cost automation in THIS repository. It does not authorize website writes or source-data redistribution.

For an engineering error: reproduce, identify cause, fix, regression-test and continue. For absent data: inspect known sources, use a permitted equivalent or explicit fallback/new version, retain the gap and continue unaffected work. For exhausted training/search budget: report the completed campaign, do not quietly extend a search on exposed outcomes. For owner-only account permissions or unavailable permitted durable storage: request the narrow action once with exact evidence and continue independent work. Never delete inconvenient evidence, weaken a check, fake an acquisition, conceal failure or fabricate a winner to achieve DONE.

Respect current branch protections, secrets, provider rules and paid limits. No force-push or destructive reset. Determine allowed storage/content before publishing. Raw licensed odds and personal website tickets do not belong in this public research repository. Preserve authentic private inputs privately only where authorized no-cost durable access is available.

## 7. Website-consumable acceptance

Do not stop at a favorable score table. A candidate package must include:

- Versioned inference code, fitted model/config where permitted, dependency/environment lock or equivalent reproducible specification, and hashes.
- Feature manifest with provenance, time semantics, public-use limitations, acquisition availability and missing-feature policy.
- A stable deterministic interface binding player/game/team/opponent/statistic/period/scoring/model/cutoff; point summary or actual distribution; fallback/abstention reasons and timing evidence.
- Unit, leakage/adversarial, source-schema, real-input integration, repeat/restart and compatibility tests. A source checksum does not independently prove a provider value is true.
- Row-level chronological evaluation and prospective capture/evaluation when due, explicit prior exposure and failures, paired baselines, subgroup/coverage and uncertainty results.
- A read-only pinned-site consumer fixture, candidate-vs-baseline output parity checks where expected, failure fallback, bounded runtime/memory, and rollback instructions.
- A release-ready adapter/diff or integration instructions for the existing website owner, without this lab writing that repository.

Declare states separately: IMPLEMENTED, REPRODUCED, SOURCE-QUALIFIED, PRE-KICKOFF-RECORDED, EVALUATED, SCIENTIFICALLY-QUALIFIED, INTEGRATION-READY, and actually deployed by the website owner. Each state needs its own evidence. Pending future outcomes can be honestly pending while functional automation is complete. Do not wait for invented winners or claim a future run already happened.
